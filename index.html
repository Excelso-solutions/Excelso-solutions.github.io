    8. Engineering Tools

    A collection of standalone engineering applications developed to support mechanical design and engineering calculations. Each tool is available online and can also be downloaded as a standalone HTML file. The source code can be inspected and adapted for individual engineering needs.

    Thermal Fit Calculator

    Shaft–Housing Thermal Clearance and Interference Analysis. A standalone engineering calculator for evaluating thermal expansion, clearance and interference conditions in cylindrical shaft–hole assemblies. Includes ISO fit tolerances, material properties, contact pressure, stress estimation and temperature-range visualization.

    Thermal Expansion ISO Fits Contact Pressure Stress Analysis
    Open Calculator Download HTML

    Calculation Model — Important Notes

    The calculator uses a simplified analytical model based on isotropic, linearly elastic cylindrical components and uniform temperature and contact pressure assumptions.

    Edge effects, surface roughness, axial loads, centrifugal forces, creep and stress relaxation are not included. Results should be treated as engineering estimates and independently verified for critical applications.
